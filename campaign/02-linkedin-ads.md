# LinkedIn Ad Copy — All 4 Segments

Format: LinkedIn Sponsored Content (single image). Character limits:
Introductory text ~600 (first ~150 shown before "…see more"), Headline
~70 for full visibility on most placements, Description ~100 shown
reliably. Two variants per segment (`a1`/`a2`) for testing — same offer,
different opening hook. Swap in `utm_content=li_{variant}` on each link
per the convention in `00-foundations.md`.

Priority order matches platform fit from `01-audience-targeting.md`:
Job Loss and Early Retirement are the primary LinkedIn segments; Divorce
and Inheritance are included for completeness but are low-priority/
low-budget tests on this platform (Facebook is the primary platform for
those two — see `03-facebook-ads.md`).

**All copy below is a draft pending compliance approval — do not launch
before sign-off (see `00-foundations.md`).**
