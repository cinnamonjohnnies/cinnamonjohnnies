# Compliance Submission: LinkedIn Month 1

What this batch covers, submit as one package rather than piecemeal so
the whole month can run without a mid-month compliance gap.

## What's included

1. **12 organic LinkedIn posts** (`08-linkedin-content-calendar-month1.md`),
   3x/week for four weeks, built as LinkedIn document (carousel) posts
   rather than single images, LinkedIn's own engagement data favors that
   format heavily over a single static image. Each post is shown as it
   will appear in-feed (profile row, caption, disclosure line, hashtags,
   swipeable document preview) plus every individual slide in full, so
   the complete content is reviewable, not just the cover slide.
2. **LinkedIn ad copy, all 4 segments** (`02-linkedin-ads.md`), 2
   variants each for Job Loss & Layoff and Early Retirement, 1 variant
   each for Divorce and Inheritance, 6 ads total. Creative is photo-led
   (a real photo of Scott alongside the headline), which outperforms
   graphic-only ad creative in LinkedIn ad benchmarks, each shown as a
   full creative mockup (ad image, headline, description, and CTA as it
   will render in-feed). Job Loss is the only segment actually launching
   now (see `06-linkedin-launch-today.md`), the other three are included
   so Phase 2 (`04-budget-and-testing-plan.md`) doesn't need a second
   compliance round-trip later.

## What's deliberately not included

- **Facebook/Instagram ad copy** (`03-facebook-ads.md`), not part of
  today's LinkedIn-focused push. Submit separately whenever that
  platform is ready to build.
- **Email nurture sequences**, still on hold per the earlier decision to
  handle follow-up manually for now.

## The assembled PDF

`compliance/LinkedIn-Month-1-Compliance-Submission.pdf`, generated from
this content by `compliance/tools/build_linkedin_month1_pdf.py`. Rerun
that script after editing any post or ad copy to regenerate it.

## After approval

- Schedule the 12 posts per the calendar in `08-linkedin-content-calendar-month1.md`.
  Each post uploads as a LinkedIn document post: use the PDF at
  `campaign/assets/post-carousels/post-NN.pdf` as the document, and the
  post text from the calendar file as the caption.
- Build the Job Loss ad campaign in Campaign Manager per
  `06-linkedin-launch-today.md` Steps 3 to 5, using the ad images in
  `campaign/assets/ad-creatives/`.
- Hold the other 3 segments' ad copy as pre-approved, ready to activate
  when Phase 2 starts.
