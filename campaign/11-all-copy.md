### Post 2, Wed 9/30, Job Loss education

If you've just been laid off, here's the order that actually matters in
the first 30 days.

Confirm your severance details, how it's paid and whether it affects
unemployment eligibility. Understand your benefits deadlines, especially
health coverage; COBRA elections typically have a 60-day window, not a
60-day requirement to decide immediately.

Then stop. Nothing about your 401(k), a major purchase, or a big
financial decision needs to happen this week.

The goal of the first month isn't a plan. It's stability. The plan comes
after.

Hashtags: #JobLoss #CareerTransition #FinancialPlanning #Layoff

### Post 3, Fri 10/2, What fiduciary means

"Fiduciary" gets used a lot in this industry. Here's what it actually
means in practice.

It means I'm legally required to act in your best interest, not the
firm's, not a product manufacturer's, and not my own. Every
recommendation has to clear that bar.

It means no sales quotas and no proprietary products I'm incentivized to
push. When insurance or annuity products do carry a commission, that's
disclosed to you in writing before any recommendation, not buried in
paperwork afterward.

It's not a marketing word here. It's a legal standard, and it's the one
I'm held to on every client relationship.

### Post 4, Mon 10/5, Checkpoint Strategy Phase 2

Continuing the series: The Checkpoint Strategy's second phase.

**Phase 2: Identify and Protect.**

Before building toward the future, we stabilize the present. That means
reviewing benefits continuation, checking for insurance gaps, and
confirming cash flow can hold steady through the transition.

This is the phase most people want to skip past, they want the plan,
the strategy, the exciting part. But a plan built on an unstable
foundation doesn't hold up. Protecting what you already have comes
before growing it.

### Post 5, Wed 10/7, Early Retirement education

Claiming Social Security at 62 instead of your full retirement age can
permanently reduce your monthly benefit by roughly 25 to 30 percent.
Waiting until 70 increases it.

I see the same mistake often: claiming early simply because retirement
started early, without ever running the numbers on the alternative.

Retiring earlier than planned doesn't mean you have to claim earlier
than planned. Those are two separate decisions, and modeling them
side by side, based on your actual numbers, usually changes the answer.

### Post 7, Mon 10/12, Checkpoint Strategy Phase 3

**Phase 3: Plot Checkpoints.**

This is where the actual plan gets built, with milestones set at 30, 90,
and 180 days rather than one distant, abstract goal.

Shorter checkpoints do two things a single long-term goal can't. They
make progress visible sooner, and they create natural points to adjust
when real life doesn't match the projection, which it usually doesn't,
at least not exactly.

Coordination with your CPA or attorney happens here too, when the
situation calls for it, so the plan reflects your whole financial
picture, not just the parts that touch investments.

Hashtags: #FinancialPlanning #CheckpointStrategy #GoalSetting

### Post 8, Wed 10/14, Divorce education

A settlement's headline number and its real value are not the same
thing.

A dollar in a Roth IRA, a dollar in a traditional 401(k), and a dollar
of home equity carry different tax treatment and different liquidity.
Two settlements with the same total can leave two very different
financial pictures once that's accounted for.

Before agreeing to a proposed split, it's worth understanding the real,
after-tax value of what's actually on the table, not just the total at
the bottom of the page.

### Post 11, Wed 10/21, Inheritance education

Inherited stocks, funds, or real estate typically receive a "step-up" in
cost basis to their value on the date of death.

That single detail can significantly reduce, or eliminate, capital gains
tax if you sell. It's also one of the easiest things to lose track of if
records aren't documented early.

Almost nothing about an inheritance needs to be decided quickly. This is
one of the few things actually worth confirming and documenting sooner
rather than later.

Hashtags: #Inheritance #EstatePlanning #FinancialPlanning
