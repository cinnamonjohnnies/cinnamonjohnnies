How it works
  1. Choose Upload Speech and add your whole script. A .txt, .md or .docx file works too.
  2. Check the suggested opening, body parts and close. Select any passage to reassign it.
  3. Create the speech, then use Move lines, Split here and Undo to arrange it, and the timer to see how long it runs.
  4. Add pauses and stage directions (a gesture, a walk across the stage, holding up a prop) as their own lines. They are counted in the time but not in your script.
  5. Print it with Export PDF, or use Stage view to rehearse with large text.
