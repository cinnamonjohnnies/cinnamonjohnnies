## Email 2: Day 2
**Subject:** What a settlement number doesn't tell you
**Preview text:** After-tax value vs. headline value

Hi {{first_name}},

Two settlements with the same total dollar figure can leave you in very different financial positions, depending on what's in them. A dollar in a Roth IRA, a dollar in a traditional 401(k), and a dollar of home equity are not equivalent once taxes and liquidity are considered.

Before agreeing to a proposed split, it's worth understanding the real, after-tax value of what's on the table, not just the total.

## Email 3: Day 5
**Subject:** QDROs, in plain language
**Preview text:** What's required to actually divide a retirement account

Hi {{first_name}},

Dividing a 401(k) or pension usually requires a separate legal document called a QDRO (Qualified Domestic Relations Order), distinct from your divorce decree. Getting this drafted and processed correctly matters; mistakes here can delay or complicate the transfer.

IRAs work differently, through a transfer incident to divorce, without a QDRO.

If you're not sure which rules apply to your accounts, that's worth clarifying before things move further along.
