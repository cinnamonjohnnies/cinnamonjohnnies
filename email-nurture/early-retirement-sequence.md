## Email 3: Day 5
**Subject:** 62, full retirement age, or 70?
**Preview text:** The Social Security question, in plain terms

Hi {{first_name}},

Claiming Social Security at 62 instead of your full retirement age can permanently reduce your monthly benefit by roughly 25-30%. Waiting until 70 increases it. There's no universally "right" answer, it depends on your health, other income, and what else you're drawing down.

The mistake I see most often: claiming early simply because retirement started early, without running the numbers on the alternative.

If you haven't modeled this out, it's one of the highest-value 45 minutes you can spend before retiring.
