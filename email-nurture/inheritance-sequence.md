Hi {{first_name}},

If you inherited an IRA or 401(k), the distribution rules depend on your relationship to the original owner and whether they had already started required minimum distributions. Getting this wrong can trigger unnecessary taxes.

This is the one area of an inheritance genuinely worth confirming early, everything else in the guide can wait.

## Email 3: Day 5
**Subject:** Why the "step-up" matters
**Preview text:** A tax detail worth understanding before you sell anything

Hi {{first_name}},

Inherited stocks, funds, and real estate typically get a "step-up" in cost basis to their value on the date of death. That can significantly reduce, or eliminate, capital gains tax if you sell.

It's worth documenting this basis now, while records are easiest to gather, even if you're not ready to decide what to do with the asset yet.
