# Email templates for Supabase

Supabase dashboard, Authentication, Emails (Templates). For each one, paste the subject and the HTML.
Site URL (Authentication, URL Configuration) must be https://talkgooder.app.

- **confirm-signup.html**: subject `One click and you are a Talk Gooder`
- **magic-link.html**: subject `Your Talk Gooder sign-in link`
- **reset-password.html**: subject `Reset your Talk Gooder password`
- **change-email.html**: subject `Confirm your new email address`

Branding update: templates now use the light logo from https://talkgooder.app/brand/logo-light.png. Deploy the site first, then paste the templates into Supabase.
