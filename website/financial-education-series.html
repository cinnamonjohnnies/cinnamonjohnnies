Four Sessions, One Hour Each

Come to one, or come to all four.

Each session stands on its own. Through weekly activities and take-home worksheets, you'll leave with practical financial tools you can use at any stage of a job search or career transition.

1

Oct 6: Your Money, Your Picture

A 10-minute look at the psychology of money, how cognitive bias shapes financial decisions without us realizing it, then building a clear financial snapshot and understanding where money is actually going.

2

Oct 13: Managing Debt and Using Credit Wisely

How credit works, what credit scores actually mean, the real cost of carrying a balance, and a practical system for managing debt while income is reduced.

3

Oct 20: Banking, Benefits, and Protecting What You Have

Using banking tools effectively, avoiding predatory financial products, and navigating the benefits landscape during a career transition, including unemployment, COBRA, and retirement accounts.

4

Oct 27: Building Toward Stability

Savings strategies, tax basics, goal setting, and creating a 90-day financial plan for whatever comes next.
